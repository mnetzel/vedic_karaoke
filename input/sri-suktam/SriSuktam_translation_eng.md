>*hiraṇyavarṇāṃ hariṇīṃ suvarṇarajatasrajām |*
>*candrāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ lakṣmīṃ jātavedo ma āvaha ||1||*

O Jātavedo (Agni as the sacrificial fire), invoke for me this Lakṣmī,  
who has a golden complexion, is beautiful, and is adorned with garlands of gold and silver.
She is like the Moon with a golden aura; she is Lakṣmī, the embodiment of Śrī.  
O Jātavedo, please invoke this Lakṣmī for me.

**hiraṇyavarṇāṃ** → "gold-colored / of golden hue"

-   _hiraṇya_ = gold
-   _varṇa_ = color, hue

**hariṇīṃ** → "a doe / doe-like / graceful like a doe"

-   _hariṇī_ = doe (a symbol of gentleness and grace)

**suvarṇarajatasrajām** → "adorned with garlands of gold and silver"

-   _suvarṇa_ = gold
-   _rajata_ = silver
-   _sraj_ = garland, wreath

**candrāṃ** → "like the moon / moon-like"

**hiraṇmayīṃ** → "golden / made of gold / filled with golden radiance"

-   _hiraṇmaya_ = golden, gold-like, filled with gold

**lakṣmīṃ** → "Lakṣmī"

**jātavedo** → "O Jātavedo (Agni, the knower of all births / the all-knowing one)"

**ma** → "for me / me"

**āvaha** → "bring / invoke / lead here"

>*aśvapūrvāṃ rathamadhyāṃ hastinādaprabodhinīm |*
>*śriyaṃ devīmupahvaye śrīrmā devī juṣatām ||3||*

O Jātavedo, I invoke for myself this Śrī (Lakṣmī),
who stands in the middle of the chariot, with horses before her,
and whose arrival is announced by the sound of trumpeting elephants.
May this goddess Śrī, the embodiment of auspicious prosperity,
be gracious toward me and bless me.

**aśvapūrvāṃ** → "she who has horses in front / preceded by horses"

- _aśva_ = horse
- _pūrvā_ = preceding, in front

**rathamadhyāṃ** → "she who is in the middle of the chariot"

- _ratha_ = chariot
- _madhya_ = middle, center

**hastinādaprabodhinīm** → "she whose coming is announced by the sound of elephants / awakened by the roar or trumpeting of elephants"

- _hastin_ = elephant
- _nāda_ = sound, roar, trumpeting
- _prabodhinī_ = she who is awakened / announced / made known

**śriyam** → "Śrī / auspicious prosperity / Lakṣmī"

**devīm** → "goddess"

**upahvaye** → "I invoke / call / invite"

- _upa_ = toward, near
- _hvaye_ (from √hve) = I call, I invoke

**śrīḥ** → "Śrī (auspiciousness, divine abundance)"

**mā** → "me / for me"

**devī** → "goddess"

**juṣatām** → "may she be gracious / may she bless / may she accept with favor"

- from √_juṣ_ = to enjoy, favor, accept

- _varṇa_ = color

> *mahādevyai ca vidmahe viṣṇupatnī ca dhīmahi |*
> *tanno lakṣmīḥ pracodayāt*

May we know Mahādevī,  
and meditate upon the one who is the consort of Viṣṇu.  
May this Lakṣmī inspire and guide us.  
  
**mahādevyai** → "to the great goddess / Mahādevī"  
  
- _mahā_ = great  
- _devī_ = goddess  
  
**ca** → "and"  
  
**vidmahe** → "we know / may we know"  
  
- from √_vid_ = to know, to understand  
  
**viṣṇupatnīm** → "the consort of Viṣṇu"  
  
- _viṣṇu_ = Viṣṇu  
- _patnī_ = wife, consort  
  
**ca** → "and"  
  
**dhīmahi** → "we meditate / contemplate"  
  
- from √_dhī_ = understanding, meditation, contemplation  
  
**tat** → "that"  
  
**naḥ** → "us / our"  
  
**lakṣmīḥ** → "Lakṣmī"  
  
**pracodayāt** → "may she impel / inspire / guide"  
  
- from √_cod_ = to urge, impel, set in motion


---
> *oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ*

Om. Peace, peace, peace.  
  
**oṃ** → "Om / the primordial vibration / source sound"  
  
**śāntiḥ** → "peace / calm / stillness / absence of disturbance"  
  
(triple repetition)
